Our Story

Feels like Ajji Mane — right in the city

Santhrupthii Hotel is a beloved nati style nonveg restaurant near Kanakadasa Shooting Circle on Double Road, RR Nagar. Known for its home-style cooking, generous portions, and the kind of warmth that makes you feel like you're at your grandmother's kitchen.

Guests come for the garlic chicken and stay for the kaal soup. Every dish is cooked fresh, with real spices and no shortcuts. Budget-friendly, family-friendly, and always crowded — which tells you everything.
